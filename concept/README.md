# Styling notes

- Use `Cormorant Garamond` for large editorial headings and `Inter` for interface/body text.
- Keep the main canvas near-black, not pure black.
- Use rose and warm gold sparingly for highlights; use muted green for research diagrams and active states.
- Use Pakistani architectural geometry and floral line art as subtle accents, not as repeating wallpaper.
- Make research diagrams accessible and legible; don't bake labels into raster images where possible.
